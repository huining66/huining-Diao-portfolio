Methods and limitations

Portfolio variance uses the covariance formula. DCF discounts five projected cash flows plus a Gordon-growth terminal value. A/B testing reports the absolute conversion difference and pooled z statistic. VaR scales annual volatility by the square root of 252. All six tools are illustrative, AI-assisted demonstrations, not investment recommendations or empirical studies.
