01 / Verified out-of-sample evaluation

Predicting risk. Explaining drivers.

The business problem is to prioritize retention outreach without treating every customer as equally likely to leave. I compare logistic regression with XGBoost using the same stratified holdout and examine feature contributions with SHAP.

Metrics from a single stratified 80/20 split, random_state=42, not cross-validated. ROC AUC +0.0067 and average precision +0.0317 versus this baseline. At the default 0.5 threshold, XGBoost has higher precision (68.3% versus 63.6%) but lower recall (51.3% versus 55.1%); a retention team would need to consider the cost of missed churners before selecting the threshold. Model selection should also consider stability, fairness and calibration.

04 / Implementation & limitations

Reproduce the results

Source: IBM's public demonstration Telco Customer Churn CSV. One-hot encoding, logistic regression with standardized features, and XGBoost with fixed training parameters. The held-out split is stratified to preserve the class distribution. The code generates a comparison CSV and SHAP plot.

No time-based validation, hyperparameter search, uncertainty intervals or intervention experiment was performed. This is a reproducible learning and research demonstration, not production-ready churn scoring.
