Precision: 60.7% at threshold 0.5. Majority-class baseline accuracy: 73.7%.

Method

Seven predictors: tenure, monthly charges, two contract indicators, fiber optic service, online security and tech support. Fit logistic regression with batch gradient descent on training records only. No customer ID used as a predictor.

Business use and limitations

Rank customers for retention outreach; select thresholds based on intervention costs and benefits. This is a retrospective illustrative dataset, not a temporal validation or production model. Prospective feature availability, calibration, fairness and intervention effectiveness require further evaluation.
