# Gold Market Visualization

**Financial markets · Visual analytics · Information design**

## Objective

Improve the readability of gold spot price information without compromising the integrity of the underlying data.

## Original visualization

[Source: BullionVault gold price chart](https://www.bullionvault.com/gold-price-chart.do)

![Original gold price visualization](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/179c605c-72fc-46fa-90a2-783bb965cd89)

The original chart provides detailed price information, but dense short-term fluctuations and competing visual elements can make the broader trend difficult to identify.

## Analytical approach

- Evaluate visual hierarchy, trend visibility and information density.
- Gather exploratory qualitative feedback from two participants.
- Prioritize key price metrics, reduce visual clutter and selectively annotate meaningful movements.

## Redesigned visualization

![Final gold price visualization redesign](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6b4fc308-5344-4ff9-b219-6b4bc6e96a5a)

| Design dimension | Redesign strategy |
| --- | --- |
| Trend visibility | Emphasize the overall price direction |
| Financial metrics | Give key information greater prominence |
| Visual hierarchy | Separate primary and supporting information |
| Annotations | Highlight meaningful movements selectively |

## Findings and limitations

Participants identified trend visibility and information hierarchy as opportunities for improvement. The redesign addresses those observations, but the small qualitative sample does not establish measurable improvements in interpretation speed or accuracy. Any smoothing or aggregation must be disclosed to avoid obscuring volatility.

This academic-origin case study demonstrates financial information design, not investment performance or a production trading tool. ChatGPT assisted with portfolio editing.
